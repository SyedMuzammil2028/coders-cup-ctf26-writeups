## Original challenge statement

Hogwarts has always relied on its enchanted towers to carry messages safely across the castle grounds.

Last night, the castle's monitoring system detected an unfamiliar signal. Shortly afterward, a restricted memory vanished from the archives.

The wardens recovered a network capture, but the records are crowded with ordinary communications.

Somewhere inside the traffic lies the identity of the false tower, the device that trusted it, and the memory that was taken.

Reconstruct what happened and recover the missing secret.
