Challenge Name: The Last Newline
Category: Cryptography
Difficulty: Medium
Points: 500

The Keeper's Vault has been sealed after a mysterious maintenance incident.

The archivist claims that the recovery document is authentic, yet its SHA-256 checksum no longer matches. To make matters worse, an important access credential has disappeared.

Fortunately, the archive remembers its past.

Investigate the files, explore their revision history, and recover the evidence needed to unlock the vault.

*Not everything that looks identical is truly the same.*
