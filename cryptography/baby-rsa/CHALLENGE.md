## Original challenge statement

An apprentice cryptographer has built an RSA encryption system to protect a secret message.

Unfortunately, he seems to have forgotten which numbers should remain private.

Connect to the service, inspect the RSA parameters, and recover the hidden message.

Sometimes, even strong mathematics cannot save a careless implementation.

Connect with `nc <HOST> <PORT>`.
