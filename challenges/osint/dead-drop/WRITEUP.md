Interpret “the heart of the internet” as Reddit and search for `hikari07.exe`.
The username `u/Jealous_Gain_9068` is also visible at the top left of the
provided image.

## Step 2 — Investigate the Reddit profile

It links to a specific Instagram comment.

public download.

contains the word `flag`. The account replies with the flag:
