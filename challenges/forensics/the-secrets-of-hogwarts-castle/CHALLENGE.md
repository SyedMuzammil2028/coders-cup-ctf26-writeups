## Original challenge statement

Investigate four recovered Hogwarts Castle photographs. Metadata and an
appended archive conceal a message left by the former keeper. Trace the
evidence, recover the current archive key, and decrypt the hidden record.

Start your private archive instance, open its gallery, and download the
original photographs for offline analysis. Submit the flag as `ACM{...}`.
