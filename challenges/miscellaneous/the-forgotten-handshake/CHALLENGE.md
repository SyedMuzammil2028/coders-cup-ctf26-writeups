## Original challenge statement

A wireless network once used within the castle has left behind a captured trace of its communications.

The network is no longer accessible, but the capture may contain enough information to recover its forgotten password.

Examine the provided `.cap` file, investigate the wireless authentication traffic, and recover the key that once protected the network.

**Flag Format:** `ACM{recovered_password}`
