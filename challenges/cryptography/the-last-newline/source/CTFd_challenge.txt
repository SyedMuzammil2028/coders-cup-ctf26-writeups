## The Last Newline

The Keeper's Vault has been sealed following an unusual maintenance incident.

The archivist insists that the recovery document was certified before the incident. Yet the document currently stored in the archive fails its integrity check.

To complicate matters, an important access record has disappeared.

The archive preserves more than its latest files. Investigate the records, recover the required evidence, and restore access to the vault.

**Your objective:** Submit the authentic recovery document, recover the missing credential, and identify the integrity discrepancy to unlock the vault.

*The archivist trusts fingerprints, not appearances.*
