## Original challenge statement

Deep within the forbidden archives of Hogwarts, an ancient transmission lies sealed behind a forgotten enchantment.

The message has passed through several layers of transformation, Fortunately, the last keeper left a warning:

"Eight bits form a character, but the message is not as it appears"

A second note, barely visible reads:

"Remove the familiar encoding that wraps the message, then let the alphabet travel thirteen places to return home."

Break the vault's seal, reverse the enchantments in the correct order, and recover the hidden secret.
