The Goblin's Debt
400        Medium
Deep within an enchanted forest, a cunning goblin guards an ancient secret known as The Secret Record.

The goblin demands 120 gold coins before he will surrender his treasure. Unfortunately, the forest offers only 23 coins per journey

He is good with numbers, yet strangely careless about where they come from.
Will you spend an eternity collecting gold, or find another way to settle the debt?

Objective: Settle the goblin's debt and retrieve the hidden flag.



hint1:             (0 points)
The forest isn't the only place worth exploring.

Sometimes, the most interesting secrets lie in the conversation between your browser and the goblin's ledger.



hint2:            (20 points)
Open your browser's Developer Tools and inspect the Network tab while depositing coins.

The goblin records what he receives, but does he actually verify how much gold you collected?
